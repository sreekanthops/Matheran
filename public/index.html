🌤️ Essential Tips
🌡️
Temperature: 15–30°C. Carry a light jacket for evenings.
🌧️
Monsoon (Jun–Sep): Heavy rains. Waterproof bags + raincoat essential.
🐛
Leeches: Active in monsoon on trails. Carry salt or lemon.
🚗
No Vehicles: Matheran is auto-free. Walk, horse, or toy train only.
💧
Hydration: Carry 2L water/person for treks.
📵
Network: Weak signal. Download offline maps before arriving.
💵
Cash: Most stalls don't accept UPI. Carry ₹2,000–3,000/person.
🐒
Monkeys: Keep food inside bags — they snatch fast!
